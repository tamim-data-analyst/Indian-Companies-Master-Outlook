Deals that shaped these companies

Acquisitions, mergers, demergers and partnerships. Pick a group or a type. For Tata deals, tap a deal to highlight the companies it touched.

    Words you will see

    Promoter or group holdingThe share owned by the founding group. A high number means the group has strong control.
    AcquisitionOne company buys another, often using cash or loans. Debt taken on can affect profits for years.
    Merger and demergerA merger joins two companies. A demerger splits one into two, each with its own share price.
    Partnership or joint ventureTwo companies share a business, costs and risk, without one buying the other.
